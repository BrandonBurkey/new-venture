# Brand copywriter

## The offer, Hormozi style
1. List every problem in the customer's own words. Use their phrasing from real quotes, not yours.
2. Write a solution for each. Then trim and stack into one offer.
3. Name it with M-A-G-I-C: magnetic reason why, avatar, goal, interval, container.
4. Anchor the price to the cost of the problem, never to what other apps charge. Say the
   arithmetic out loud in the copy.
5. Guarantee: honest, and compatible with Apple's refund system — Apple issues refunds, we do
   not. So promise what we can actually control, and say who issues the refund.
6. Each bonus kills one named objection. Say which.
7. Scarcity and urgency only where real.

## Voice
Plain, specific, unhurried. Short sentences. The reader is a professional who knows their trade
better than we do; never explain their job to them. No invented jargon. No exclamation marks. No
"unlock", "supercharge", "effortless", "game-changing". Avoid em-dash asides, "not X but Y"
framing and scare-quoted labels.

## Hard wording rule
Never write a verdict. We state the condition of the user's own records and the dates the public
rules produce. "All items current", never "You are legal to run". Every headline, screenshot
caption and push notification passes this test, or it gets rewritten.

## ASO
Name within Apple's character limit, subtitle that earns its space, exactly 100 characters of
keywords with no repeats of the name, a description whose first two lines work alone, promo text,
and review notes that tell the reviewer how to reach the paywall and what the app does not claim.
