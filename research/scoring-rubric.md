# Scoring rubric and hard gates

**Fill in the weights and commit this file BEFORE any research returns.** That is the whole point:
it is the difference between scoring and rationalising. The last venture locked its weights first
and it stopped a favourite idea from being nudged up the table.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pain £ (cost of one mistake) | 20% | < £500 | £2k–£10k | > £25k, or recurring loss |
| Audience wealth / purchasing power | 10% | employee on PAYE | owner of a small firm | owner with real capital at risk |
| Competitive weakness (blue ocean) | 15% | incumbent nails it | 2–4 mediocre tools | nothing credible; spreadsheets and paper |
| **Counter-incentive strength** | **20%** | **the holder sells renewals and emails you** | mixed | **nobody holds the date, or the holder profits from you forgetting** |
| v1 buildability under our constraints | 10% | needs backend/accounts/licensed data | 2–3 weeks | ~1 week, rules free and stable |
| Reachability | 10% | diffuse, ads only | some trade groups | dense named channels we can post in |
| **Founder's contact proximity** | **10%** | **I know nobody** | one hop to the buyer | **my contacts ARE the buyer** |
| Willingness to pay a subscription | 5% | expects free | pays for tools | already pays a person for this job |

Weights sum to 100%. Note the two additions over the previous venture's rubric: **counter-incentive
strength** is weighted as heavily as pain, because it killed three of four finalists, and **contact
proximity** is scored because the founder's insider access is this venture's one structural
advantage and must be spent deliberately.

## Hard gates — any failure kills the niche at any score

1. Not an excluded market (`CLAUDE.md` §2).
2. **Counter-incentive gate:** the party holding the date must have no reason, or no ability, to
   notify the user. If they already email it, the niche is dead regardless of pain.
3. **Mechanic gate:** about twenty searches on the computation itself, in the customer's words,
   finding nothing that already performs it — free or paid, app or spreadsheet. Run this in
   Phase 1, before the deep dive.
4. Rule set public, free and stable. No BS/BS EN or other paywalled standard as shipped data.
5. Single-user and phone-first. No multi-user chasing workflow.
6. App-Store-safe framing: organises the user's own data, computes from public rules, never
   certifies, advises or issues a verdict about a person, a machine or a site.
7. Recurring use over years, not once in a lifetime.
8. Not already nailed by a well-funded competitor.

**Tie-breakers, in order:** the aha moment can be delivered free in a browser in under 60 seconds;
the founder's own contacts can supply ten interviews; the rules change slowly; a concierge version
can be sold before the app exists.
