# Scoring rubric and weighting (set BEFORE seeing scout results)

Locked 2026-10-19, before any scout returned, so the weights cannot be bent to fit a
favourite idea.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pain $ (cost of one mistake) | 25% | < $500 | $2k–$10k | > $25k, or recurring loss |
| Audience wealth / purchasing power | 15% | median household | $150k+ income or $250k+ asset | $300k+ income or $1M+ asset at risk |
| Competitive weakness (blue ocean) | 20% | well-funded incumbent nails it | 2–4 mediocre apps, free portals | no credible app; spreadsheets and paper |
| v1 buildability under our constraints | 15% | needs backend/accounts/licensed data | 2–3 weeks, some rules entry | ~1 week, rules free and stable |
| Reachability (named, cheap channels) | 15% | diffuse, ad-only | some forums, no clear hub | dense named forums/associations/groups we can post in |
| Willingness to pay a subscription | 10% | expects free | pays for tools, haggles | already pays pros/software for this |

**Hard gates (any failure = kill, regardless of score)**
1. Not aviation; not a previously rejected niche (unless new contrary evidence is shown).
2. Rule set is public, free and stable — no licensed/paywalled data (NFPA, AWS, ASTM full text, purchased datasets).
3. Single-user, phone-first. No backend, no accounts, no multi-user office workflow, no marketplace.
4. App-Store-safe: organizes the user's own data, computes from public rules; never certifies, diagnoses, advises or issues a verdict. Standard of care: we renamed "Airworthy today?" → "All items current?".
5. Recurring use: the user needs it repeatedly for years, not once in a lifetime. One-shot events cannot carry a subscription.
6. Not already nailed by a well-funded competitor.

**Tie-breakers, in order:** (a) the aha moment can be delivered free in a browser in under
60 seconds; (b) the audience can be reached without paid ads; (c) the rules change slowly
(low maintenance); (d) a concierge version can be sold before the app exists.
